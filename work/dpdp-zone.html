Filling a room for Privacy by Design

A brand-new LinkedIn page. A ticketed, application-only summit on India's DPDP Act. Two months to fill it. Here is how it went.

The challenge

Fill a room, not with a general conference crowd, but with founders who will have to comply with India's DPDP Act, and the investors and enterprise leaders who will start asking them about it. The page was new, the ticket was paid, the date was fixed, and the partner roster kept growing while the campaign was already live.

The work

One system, every format

I owned the campaign end to end and built all 78 posts on one system, so every format stayed consistent and fast to produce even as the agenda and roster moved.

  1. 01

    One content calendar

    Phased across awareness, agenda, speaker, partner and conversion posts.

  2. 02

    Verify every source

    Nothing about a partner, speaker or sponsor published without reading their own page first.

  3. 03

    Repeatable structures

    Caption templates that kept every post distinct, never reusing an opening line.

  4. 04

    Format to intent

    Static to announce, carousel to explain, video for energy, ads for reach.

  5. 05

    One living document

    Every caption, note and house rule in a single shared reference.

Across the two months, that produced:

  • Awareness, agenda and conversion posts across the calendar
  • 20+ individually researched partner announcements
  • Speaker announcements and ready-to-post speaker captions
  • LinkedIn ad copy for six ad sets
  • Carousels, posters, the event key visual and motion graphics
  • A bylined thought-leadership article for the host

Selected campaign creative.

The insights

What worked

  • Partner and speaker announcements travelled furthest. Naming what each one brings gets them to reshare.
  • Exclusivity beat information. Posts about who the room was for outperformed posts about the agenda.
  • Event week drove discovery. Search appearances for the page jumped 164%.

Three of the campaign's carousel posts. Swipe, or use the arrows, to move through each deck.

The results

What two months delivered

The room filled. On 18 September, 350 people came to the Radisson Blu Atria: 250 founders, 35 investors, and an ecosystem of more than 40 partners. My work was the LinkedIn layer that built the event's audience and kept the right people paying attention. Here is what that delivered.
