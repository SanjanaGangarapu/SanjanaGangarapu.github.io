The challenge

Privacy by Design 2026 needed to reach founders preparing for the DPDP Act, as well as investors and enterprise leaders who would ask about their data practices. The LinkedIn page was new, the tickets were paid for, and the event date was fixed. Meanwhile, the speaker and partner rosters kept changing as the campaign ran.

The work

One system, every format

I own the LinkedIn campaign end-to-end, creating 78 posts within a consistent content system. That made it possible to keep publishing as the agenda and roster changed.

  1. 01

    One content calendar

    One calendar took the page from awareness to conversion, step by step.

  2. 02

    Verify every source

    I read every partner's and speaker's own page before writing a word about them.

  3. 03

    Repeatable structures

    Simple caption templates kept the voice consistent, and no two posts opened the same way.

  4. 04

    Format to intent

    Static posts to announce, carousels to explain, video for energy, ads for reach.

  5. 05

    One living document

    Every caption, note and house rule lived in one shared document.

Across the two months, that produced:

  • Awareness, agenda and conversion posts across the calendar
  • 20+ individually researched partner announcements
  • Speaker announcements and ready-to-post speaker captions
  • LinkedIn ad copy for six ad sets
  • Carousels, posters, the event key visual and motion graphics
  • A bylined thought-leadership article for the host

Selected campaign creative.

The insights

What worked

  • Partner and speaker announcements travelled furthest. Naming what each one brings gets them to reshare.
  • Exclusivity beat information. Posts about who the room was for outperformed posts about the agenda.
  • Event week drove discovery. Search appearances for the page jumped 164%.

Three of the campaign's carousel posts. Swipe, or use the arrows, to move through each deck.

The results

What two months delivered

The room filled. On 18 September, 350 people came to the Radisson Blu Atria: 250 founders, 35 investors, and an ecosystem of more than 40 partners. My work was the LinkedIn layer that built the event's audience and kept the right people paying attention. Here is what that delivered.
