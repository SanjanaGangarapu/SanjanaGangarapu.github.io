A year-round voice for The Autonomous

A two-day summit on software-defined autonomy, and a LinkedIn page that had to stay alive the other 363 days. Seven months of content, one voice. Here is how it went.

The challenge

The Autonomous brings the industry together for two days in Vienna. My job was the rest of the year: keeping a specialist audience of engineers, executives and partners engaged on LinkedIn while the programme was still being written. Speakers landed month by month, the agenda shifted, and every announcement had to sound like one voice.

The work

One system, every format

I owned the LinkedIn content end-to-end for seven months: 46 posts, nine newsletters, four carousels and five films, all briefed, written and published on one system so the feed read as a single voice.

The insights

What worked

  • Paid bought reach, organic built the audience. Sponsored impressions overtook organic from May, but every one of the 353 new followers arrived organically.
  • The run-up compounds. Organic impressions roughly tripled between February and August as the agenda, speakers and the Disruptors' Track landed.
  • Search became discovery. In the final week the page appeared in 456 searches, up 22.6%, with people looking for the event by name.
